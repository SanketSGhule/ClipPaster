ClipPaster Chrome Extension

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

ClipPaster is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you use the ClipPaster browser extension.

2. Data Collection and Usage

ClipPaster does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or keystrokes.

  • Text Snippets & Settings: All text chunks and shortcut preferences you configure are stored locally on your device or synced across your personal browser sessions using Google Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync API. No data is ever transmitted to any external third-party servers.
  • Keystrokes: Keystroke listening is performed entirely locally on your machine within the active tab to detect your configured hotkey combination. Keystrokes are never logged, recorded, or transmitted.

3. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Used solely to save and synchronize your custom snippet text and modifier preferences across your signed-in Chrome instances.
  • <all_urls> (Content Scripts): Required exclusively to allow you to insert your configured text snippets into input fields, textareas, and rich-text editors on websites of your choice when pressing your hotkey.

4. Third-Party Services

ClipPaster does not utilize third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support tab.
