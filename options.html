Why not plain Ctrl + 1-9?

In Google Chrome and Chromium browsers, Ctrl + 1 through Ctrl + 9 is a hardcoded browser accelerator that switches tabs. To solve this without switching tabs, ClipPaster uses Ctrl + Shift + [0-9] by default, or you can pick your preferred modifier below!
